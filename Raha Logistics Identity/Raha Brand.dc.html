01 — Identity

Every truck
runs full.

Raha is a freight network for Ethiopia. It finds the unused space on trucks already moving between cities and fills it with cargo that needs to go the same way. The identity is built from that one idea: a load, a match, and a direction.

RAHA
02 — The Load Mark

An R assembled from freight

Three solid blocks on a 24-unit grid. Read together they form the letter R. Read apart they describe the product: what the truck already carries, the cargo Raha matches into the spare space, and the road ahead.

Grid
24 × 24 units. 2-unit channels separate every block.
The stem — load on board
8 t already in the truck, Addis Ababa to Hawassa.
The bowl — the match
800 kg of cargo fills the 2 t of spare capacity.
The leg — direction
A forward slant at 56°, the angle of a loaded ramp.

05 — Color

Steel, paper and ink

One accent. Steel is the color of the trailer frame and the dispatch board, not of a software brand. Status is carried by fill, outline and weight, so no state depends on red or green.

07 — The LoadLine

Capacity, drawn the same everywhere

Ten cells per truck. Ink cells are freight on board, outlined cells are space for sale, steel cells are a Raha match. It appears on job cards, the match board, the trailer and the driver's home screen.
