01 — THE MARK

Cargo meets capacity.

Raha's symbol is two half-discs on a shared axis. The lower half is the truck already on the road with its load. The upper half is the cargo waiting for a ride. Offset, they read as forward motion. Together, they make one full trip.

How the mark is built

48-unit grid. Two radius-16 half-discs, offset 8 units horizontally and separated by a 4-unit lane. No letterform, no truck, no pin.

Capacity
The lower half sits ahead, already moving. A truck on the Addis–Hawassa corridor with 8 t aboard.
Cargo
The upper half trails, waiting to join. 800 kg of coffee sacks at a Bole warehouse.
The lane
The 4-unit gap is the shared route. When a match is made, the halves slide into alignment in motion.
