How a match works

No new trucks, no new roads. Raha reads where capacity is already going and puts cargo on it.

01
Trucks post their space

Drivers, fleets and brokers mark where a truck is going and how much is free. One tap in the driver app, or a Telegram reply.

02
Businesses post cargo

Pickup, drop, weight, volume, ready time. Raha shows trucks that fit, with the driver, plate and verification status of each.

03
Delivered with proof

Pickup photo, corridor check-ins, and a delivery PIN from the receiver. Everyone sees the same record.
